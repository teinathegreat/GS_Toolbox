      ℹ️
      Extension during active stay
      For Airbnb and Booking.com extensions requested while the stay is in progress, please take payment by phone. Also create a workflow advising Finance that partial payment was taken by phone while the balance will flow through the portal.

      Change of dates via portal
      For Airbnb and Booking.com change of dates, please ask the guest to request the change in the portal. We will accept it on our side. This ensures their system calculates the correct amount.
