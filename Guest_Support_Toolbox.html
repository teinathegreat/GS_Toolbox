Call Script
Next Steps
  • Full refund of Bachcare service charges (cleaning & linen)
  • Rental charges — not refunded
  • Booking fee — not refunded
  • If property re-lets: possible additional refund less $60 admin fee
Initial Response
Hi [guest name], thank you for calling. I can hear this is a difficult situation, and I want to be as helpful as I can. I do need to be upfront with you about where things stand under the direct booking policy. As your booking is within 30 days of check-in, the rental charges are unfortunately non-refundable at this stage. What is refunded are the Bachcare service charges — cleaning and linen. The booking fee is also not refundable. However — and I want to make sure I mention this — we will re-list the property for your cancelled dates. If we're able to re-book those dates, we'll pass back any recovered rental amount to you, less a $60 administration fee. So there is a possibility of getting more back, though I can't guarantee it. Would you like to talk through your options before making a final decision?
If The Guest Says...
💬Guest: So I'm losing all the rental money?
▼
🎧Agent: Under the policy, the rental charges are non-refundable at this point — I'm really sorry about that. The cleaning and linen fees are refunded. And if the property re-lets for your dates, we will refund whatever we recover from that re-booking, less the $60 admin fee. It's not guaranteed, but we will actively try to re-let it.
💬Guest: What does re-let mean exactly?
▼
🎧Agent: Re-letting means we put the property back on the market for your cancelled dates. If another guest books through Bachcare, you may get some of your rental back.

If you only paid a deposit: up to 30% of what the new guest pays.

If you paid the full rental upfront: up to 100% of what the new guest pays.

Either way, a $60 admin fee is deducted first, and the refund is only processed after the new guest has stayed — and only if the amount after the fee is more than $60.
💬Guest: How quickly will I get the re-let refund if the property re-books?
▼
🎧Agent: Once the property re-lets and the stay has been completed, we'll process the refund back to you as quickly as possible — less the $60 admin fee. We'll contact you directly when that happens. Unfortunately we can't give you an exact timeframe as it depends on whether and when the property re-books.
💬Guest: If the property doesn't get rebooked, can I just keep my booking?
▼
🎧Agent: Unfortunately no — once a cancellation is confirmed and processed, it can't be reversed. The booking will be closed off on our end and the dates re-listed. It's important to be sure before we proceed, which is why I'd encourage you to take a moment and confirm you definitely want to go ahead with the cancellation.
💬Guest: Can you waive the policy?
▼
🎧Agent: I completely understand why you're asking, and I'm sorry I don't have better news. The policy is based on the timing of the cancellation and isn't something I'm able to override. I want to be transparent — if this is a genuine emergency or exceptional circumstance such as a serious illness or bereavement, I can absolutely raise that with the property owner on your behalf. However, if the reason is simply that the dates are no longer needed, that isn't something we'd escalate to the owner as it wouldn't be fair to them. In that case, the policy as outlined is what applies.
💬Guest: What if I just don't show up instead of cancelling?
▼
🎧Agent: I'd strongly advise against that — if you don't cancel and simply don't arrive, you'd still lose the rental charges but we also wouldn't be able to re-list the property, which removes any chance of recovering anything. Cancelling gives you the best possible outcome under the circumstances.
💬Guest: Can I change the dates instead?
▼
🎧Agent: Unfortunately at this stage we're not able to offer a date change — the policy for this booking applies as outlined. I'm sorry I can't offer more flexibility here. If you'd like to proceed with the cancellation we can get that sorted for you.
💬Guest: Will my travel insurance cover this?
▼
🎧Agent: That's really worth checking with your insurance provider directly, as coverage varies a lot between policies. If you do have a claim, they'll likely ask for a cancellation confirmation from us — which we can absolutely provide once the cancellation is processed.
💬Guest: Can I get written confirmation of the cancellation?
▼
🎧Agent: Absolutely. Once the cancellation is confirmed on our end, we'll send a confirmation through to you. If you need it for insurance purposes or for your own records, just let us know and we'll make sure it's clear and covers everything you need.
💬Guest: It's not my fault I have to cancel — why should I lose money?
▼
🎧Agent: I completely understand your frustration, and I'm sorry you're in this position. The cancellation policy is set by the platform and applies to all bookings regardless of the reason — it's not something we can override unilaterally. What I can do is explain exactly what you're entitled to under the policy, and if there are exceptional circumstances involved, I'm happy to contact the owner on your behalf to see if they're willing to consider anything further.
💬Guest: This is a genuine emergency / serious personal or health situation.
▼
🎧Agent: I'm really sorry to hear that — thank you for letting me know.

While I do need to work within the standard cancellation policy, what I can do is reach out to the property owner directly on your behalf to explain the circumstances and ask if they're willing to consider discretion.

I want to be transparent — this is entirely at their discretion, and the outcome could be a partial refund, a credit, or a change of dates rather than a guaranteed full refund. But I'm absolutely happy to make that call and advocate for you. Would you like me to do that?
💬Guest: I want to make a complaint / speak to a manager.
▼
🎧Agent: Of course — I completely understand, and I'm sorry this hasn't been the outcome you were hoping for. I'll make sure your concern is escalated and that someone follows up with you directly. Can I take the best contact details for you and confirm the best time to reach you?
